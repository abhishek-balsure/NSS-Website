NSS Trivia Challenge

How well do you know the National Service Scheme? Answer these 15 questions to test your knowledge, earn badges, and win your certificate of completion!

Rules of the Challenge:

  • 15 Questions multiple-choice challenge.
  • 45 Seconds limit per question.
  • Get educational explanations for every answer.
  • Score 70% or more to earn a special Volunteer Badge & Certificate!
  • The quiz can be taken once per month — a fresh set of questions arrives every month.
